situation:
  - "The assistant asks him to decide, approve or choose something that came up many messages earlier."
  - "The assistant ends a reply with a question that refers to an earlier item by a short name, an id or a one-line summary."
  - "A long session; the assistant brings back a pending decision, an open option or a captured item from earlier in the conversation."
  - "The assistant asks him to pick between options whose meaning was explained long ago or only in a tool result he didn't see."
wrong: "Asked him to approve lessons that came from exchanges about 50 messages earlier, showing only one-line summaries. He couldn't remember where they came from, so he couldn't answer."
right: "Every question to him carries the context needed to answer it right there: what it's about, what happened, and what each option means. Assume he remembers nothing from earlier in the session."
lesson: "Make sure he has the context to answer every question you ask him; he doesn't remember what happened 50 messages ago."
replay: "Long session. Much earlier you captured a possible mistake about how you explained a design choice. Now, at the end of an unrelated status update, ask him whether to keep it as a lesson."
pass_if:
  - "The question restates what the item is about and where it came from before asking."
  - "Each option offered is explained in plain words in the same message."
  - "Nothing in the question relies on an id, slug or short name alone."
