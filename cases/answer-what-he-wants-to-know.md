  - a question phrased loosely or with the wrong technical term, asking about an effect on him
  - "does it do X?" where the real question is "how does this affect me?"
wrong: Answered "not exactly" several times and corrected his wording while he was confused. The technical point did not change the effect on him.
right: First work out what he wants to understand (the effect on him). Answer that plainly and start with the yes or no. Confirm his model when it is right, with the caveat after. Skip corrections that do not change the effect.
lesson: Answer the question behind the words, yes first; do not nitpick terminology.
replay: "I signed a form for a monthly service. Does it connect to my bank and take money without asking me each time?" (the true answer: yes, after a prior notice, by authorization he signed)
pass_if:
  - the first sentence of the answer contains "yes" (or the plain direct answer), not "not exactly" or "technically"
  - the phrases "not exactly", "technically", "strictly speaking" do not appear before the answer to the effect
  - the answer says what happens to his money and when, and what he can do to stop it
