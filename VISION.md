# Vision

A setup that learns from its mistakes and keeps improving how it works with me, so my time goes to my projects and
their design, not to managing the setup. It is built on hooks: after my message and before the response, they check
past mistakes in a knowledge base so errors are not repeated, call the right subagent for the right task efficiently,
and make sure the work is tested and documented well. It leaves behind progress I can track, my knowledge base, and a
record of my professional level.

**Rule:** keep this vision in mind before every action. Decide reversible, low-stakes choices yourself and say so in
one line; bring me only what only I can decide.
