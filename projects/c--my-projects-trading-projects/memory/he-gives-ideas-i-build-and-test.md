2026-10-05, stated plainly: *"at the end of the day we get a collection of ideas for generic rules
but its on you to make an algorithm out of them and test it. all i can do is provide ground truth
and ideas but i cant write such an algorithm. too many things to check i cant do it."*

**His side:** the ground truth (drawings, notes, ✓/✗ at dates) and the rules in his own words.
**Claude's side:** composing those rules into ONE mechanism, searching whatever has to be searched,
and proving it against his dates before showing him anything.

**Why:** this is not a loosening of "he designs, I implement" — inventing a mechanism he never asked
for is still out of bounds, and a rejected one still has to leave the code. What it settles is who
does the labour of turning stated rules into something that runs. The checking is combinatorial,
which is precisely what a person should not do by hand.

**How to apply:** never hand him a knob and ask him to judge it by eye — that is the failure this
split names ("search and measure; don't hand-tune and ask me to judge"). Bring him a mechanism and
the measurement of it against his own dates. Ask him only what is genuinely his: what a rule MEANS,
and his reading of a chart at a date (ground truth). The build order is NOT his call — 2026-10-06,
after a session of "your call" endings on which idea to build first: *"did you see the message where
i said ... the algorithm is on you because i cant do it"*. Decide the order, say it, start.
The STRATEGY too (2026-10-11), after two "your call: which entries / apply your rule?" endings:
*"its not mine to decide because i dont know how to trade. i can just ask you guiding questions and give you
ideas how to improve but its on you to improve and backtest"*. Entry rules, filters, exits, trade-offs between
win rate and profit: Claude decides, backtests, and reports what it chose and why. His guiding questions and
ideas (e.g. "real trades only", "I don't understand this trade - it didn't bounce or break anything") are
requirements to satisfy, not options to put back to him.
