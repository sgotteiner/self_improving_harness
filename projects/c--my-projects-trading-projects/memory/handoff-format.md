A handoff is four parts: **what we did**, **what he doesn't like** (his frustrations and corrections, in
his words), **visibility and communication in development** (how he shows me things and how I see his
screen - tools, logs, cards), and **how to make it easy for him to keep working** (what is running, what
to do first, what not to make him repeat).

**Why:** "a handoff is what we did what i dont like and visibility and communication in develop and how
to make it easy for me to keep working" - said after a handoff that was only code state and numbers. The
next session repeats his frustrations unless they are handed over, and he should not have to re-explain
how we work.

**How to apply:** every end-of-session handoff (still a message, not a file in his repo -
[[sr-lines-next-session]]) uses these four headings; the dislikes are quoted, not paraphrased away.
