When the user names a target — "backfill sangitagem", a source, a set — that scope is the instruction. Deliver all of it.

**Why:** He caught me handing back 18 of 33 sangitagem coins as a self-chosen "≥3-mention cohort" when he'd asked to backfill the group. Narrowing his scope to my own judgment produced "half results" and reads as the same failure as inventing things he didn't design — see [[feedback_user_designs]] and [[feedback_role_boundary]].

**How to apply:** Do the whole named target. If part of it looks like noise (false-positive tickers, low-signal items), FLAG it with evidence and let him decide whether to trim — never pre-trim silently. Deliver the asked thing in full, then stop.
