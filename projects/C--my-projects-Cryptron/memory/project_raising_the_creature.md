embodiment principle makes the bot refuse honestly, which marks the gap precisely.

in the logs as bugs; they're the roadmap. The 24/7 autonomous loop is deliberately deferred
until he feels the system is ready — he considers it quick to add on top of the existing
bloodstream (see [[cryptron-core-thesis]]).
