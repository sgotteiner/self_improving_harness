When working on the Cryptron vision doc, genuinely engage with and reconcile the conceptual
model before touching text — don't just mechanically apply a change. Reason through how a
refinement ripples across the whole anatomy and fix every affected spot for consistency.

**Why:** The user explicitly said "I'm very happy you understood it" after I reconciled the
experiment/anatomy taxonomy across the doc rather than editing one line. Demonstrated
understanding of the model is what they value.

**How to apply:** On Cryptron doc changes, (1) confirm the refined concept in plain terms,
(2) grep for every place the old framing appears and update all of them, (3) keep the
biological metaphor coherent. Two settled principles so far:
- The tissue/organ line is **intra-source vs cross-source** (combining within one source =
  tissue; combining across sources, e.g. Pine + CMC = organ).
- **Only atoms and senses cannot be configured/experimented** (atom = the knob; sense = raw
  perception). Everything above them — molecules, tissues, organs, the testing organ — is
  configurable *and* a valid subject of an experiment.
